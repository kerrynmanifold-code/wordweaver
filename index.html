    How to play
    • Tap letters one at a time, or drag across them.
    • Each letter must touch the last one – up, down, sideways or diagonally.
    • Words can bend and change direction.
    • Tap the last letter again to remove it. Tap an earlier letter to go back to it.
    • Correct answers lock in automatically. Use Check Word for feedback.
    • Theme words turn teal. The Golden Thread ★ turns gold with a double border.
    • Keyboard: arrow keys move, Enter/Space selects, Backspace removes, Esc clears.
